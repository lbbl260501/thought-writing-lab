# Publication, provenance and privacy

## Public material
Original summaries and critical analyses; independently authored exercises; facts and bibliographic metadata; short attributable excerpts within applicable rights; openly licensed items whose license permits redistribution.

## Keep outside public GitHub
Commercial book scans, publisher PDFs without redistribution rights, private Drive folder/file IDs, signed download links, shared-account data, Zotero SQLite files, login credentials, or other personal/nonpublic information.

## Attribution
- Record the exact source, edition/version, pages or sections **only when checked**.
- Distinguish direct quotation from paraphrase and inference.
- An open-access link does not by itself establish redistribution permission; review the actual license.

## Scientific reasoning and framing
Authors may select an evidence-backed contribution and arrange information strategically. The main claim must remain calibrated to the evidence; negative or mixed results should receive the detail necessary to interpret central claims. Do not fabricate results or citations.

## Before publishing
Check whether the repo is public; review git diffs for identifiers and secrets; verify source permissions; make educational materials independent of confidential source attachments.
