# Daily-learning instructional evidence and limits

Updated 2026-10-14. Audit scope: official/primary public explanatory sources only. Source links are references, not copied full text.

| Source | Supports | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Dunlosky et al., 2013, *Psychological Science in the Public Interest*](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/pspi/1529100612453266/) | Practice testing and distributed practice have broad evidence for learning across contexts. | A specific 60-minute regimen or 1/3/7/14-day schedule is universally optimal. |
| [The Learning Scientists, six strategies](https://www.learningscientists.org/blog/2016/8/18-1) | Evidence-based retrieval, spacing, elaboration, interleaving and example use can inform practice design. | That all six should occupy equal time or that every strategy is equally effective. |
| [EEF 2025, Metacognition & Self-Regulated Learning](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition) | Planning/monitoring/evaluation, scaffolded practice and authentic task integration are useful educational approaches. | Directly quantified effects for this particular adult research-writing program. |
| [University of Manchester, Academic Phrasebank](https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/about-academic-phrasebank/) | Genre-based academic rhetorical moves link communicative purposes with characteristic language choices. | That phrase templates alone guarantee research writing quality, or are transferable unchanged across journals. |

**Our design hypotheses:** (a) the same narrative action trained at sentence, paragraph, research and new-domain levels may become transferable; (b) private retrieval/error logs may improve metacognitive calibration; (c) 28 learning days is a practical module length. These are instructional designs to evaluate, not proven empirical results.

**Teaching corpus boundary:** Week 01 exercises and demonstrations are newly authored examples, not substantial extracts from MICCAI articles or textbooks; they often use hypothetical scientific premises. The research reasoning requirements should still be checked for disciplinary accuracy, and later paper-based lessons must cite exact source sections.
