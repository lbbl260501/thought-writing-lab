## Actual source reading and evidence status

Six accepted-paper MICCAI Society 2025 PDFs and their official bibliographic pages were examined with the scope recorded in SOURCE_AUDIT.md. The actual checked pages covered introductions and abstracts; maximum verification level is **INTRODUCTION_READ**, not FULLTEXT_AUDITED. Sources:

- miccai:2025:3770 — uncertainty-supervised evidential segmentation
- miccai:2025:3977 — edge-aware token halting
- miccai:2025:0496 — source-free cross-modality cardiac segmentation
- miccai:2025:1764 — FedAMM, arbitrary modality missingness
- miccai:2025:3902 — conformal prediction sets for segmentation
- miccai:2025:2131 — E-BayesSAM, Bayesian adaptation and interpretation

**AUTHOR_CLAIM** denotes paper authors' scientific assertions, which our linguistic work does not replicate. **ANALYST_INTERPRETATION** denotes SVO, given/new and move analysis. **TRANSFER_HYPOTHESIS** denotes use of a move in other journals/exams/research domains. Comments by public reviewers, such as E-BayesSAM novelty objections, are **REVIEWER_OPINION**, not independently proven defects. No experimental result, true learner grade or practice submission has been invented.

## Explicit limitations

1. The pilot includes only six MICCAI 2025 works. It cannot establish universal style norms for MIA, TMI, BSPC, or CET/postgraduate/IELTS/TOEFL exam writing.
2. Full methods, experiments and original review responses have not been comprehensively audited; source-authored performance assertions are not independently verified.
3. Some original teaching paraphrases intentionally compress technical detail; verify corresponding passages again before scientific reuse.
4. The local D labels overlap but are not automatically identical to the shared central move labels; HQ arbitration is required.
5. No personal learning data, private Drive URL, Zotero database, commercial source PDF or fabricated student results were published.
