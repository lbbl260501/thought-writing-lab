# D03 · 讲解与参考答案

## 任务 A

一种顺序为 **B → C → D → A**。

- B 引入 PET 和 CT 的互补观察。
- C 的 `their features` 指向前句两种模态，提出利用程度可能变化的新问题。
- D 切换到模型政策：`A model with a fixed fusion policy` 是对前述变化能否被处理的回应。
- A 的 `This variation` 应明确回指病例/病灶间特征价值变化，而不是回指固定策略本身。

另一种可行顺序为 **B → D → C → A**：先介绍固定策略再指出这种统一策略与可变任务需求之间的张力。注意在 C 中最好改为 `Yet the usefulness of each modality may differ...`，使对照更直接。

## 任务 B · 原创示例

> PET and CT provide complementary observations of metabolic activity and anatomical structure. These observations are often combined in tumor segmentation networks. Their relative contribution, however, may change with lesion appearance and surrounding tissue. It is therefore important to investigate whether fixed fusion strategies are equally suitable across cases.

该段没有声称“变化已被实验证实”，而是将其保留为值得检验的问题。可以把 `may change` 改为更具体的研究假设，但不能在没有数据时改成绝对事实。

## 任务 C · 示例

> Writing assistants can help students revise the language of an essay. Such support may make the final draft more fluent. The fluency of an assisted draft, however, does not necessarily reveal how well the student can revise independently. A useful evaluation would compare subsequent revisions completed without access to the tool.

句群没有依赖套话堆叠；`Such support`、`The fluency...`、`A useful evaluation` 依次承接前一句提出的关键概念。

## 思想迁移

新旧信息流不是让文章“显得连贯”的装饰，而是让每一句**对一个已经建立的研究对象执行新的叙事动作**。
