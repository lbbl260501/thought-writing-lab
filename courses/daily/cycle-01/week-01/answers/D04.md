# D04 · 讲解与参考答案

## 任务 A

1. **关联不能证明因果。** 如果数据仅显示相关性，可以写：`PET uptake was associated with the measured marker of lesion aggressiveness in the observed sample.` 是否有因果关系需要另行检验；此外 tracer uptake 不可在未经限定时等同于肿瘤进展。
2. **预测能力不是机制理解。** `The model predicted pose-estimation errors in the evaluated cases, but these results alone do not establish a biomechanical explanation.` 应检查是否包含变形力学、因果机制和跨条件实验。
3. **一次损失改善不等于所有患者不可替代。** `Adding the module reduced the observed training or validation loss under the reported setup.` 还需独立验证集、患者级分析和容量匹配的消融比较。

## 任务 B

H1: `Case-adaptive modality selection improves segmentation because the predictive value of each modality differs across patients.`

H2: `The observed gains arise from greater model capacity or additional optimization rather than case-specific information selection.`

可采用**匹配参数量、计算预算和训练方案**的固定双模态基线、随机门控和自适应门控对比；继续检查病例级路由是否与独立估计的模态增益一致。一个对照通常不足以证明完整机制，需要多种证据。

## 任务 C · 示例段落

> PET and CT provide complementary information, but their relative predictive value may differ across cases. Such variation could make a uniform computational policy inefficient. Alternatively, apparent benefits of adaptive fusion might simply reflect additional parameters or optimization. We therefore hypothesize that estimating case-specific modality value can improve performance under a fixed compute budget. To test this hypothesis, we would compare adaptive and fixed routing policies with matched model capacity and training procedures, while examining whether the selected routes correspond to independent modality-intervention estimates.

这段是研究方案，不是完成的实验结论。

## 要点

从“我能预测”到“我知道原因”，需要额外的证据桥梁。好的科学写作使这个桥梁可见，避免读者把推断误认为观察。
