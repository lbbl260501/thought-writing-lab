# D13 · 面向受众定位贡献的示范

以下讨论只对应课程设定的虚构数值：基线 A / 方法 B，Dice 0.840 / 0.830，100 / 68 GFLOPs。未测项目不得在任何版本中变成已证明的优势。

## 任务 1：把承诺拆出来

**A 错在双重跨越。**“real-time”需要端到端时延和实时阈值；“without compromising accuracy”与现有平均 Dice 降低不一致，亦无不劣效统计设计。可改写：

> The method reduced estimated FLOPs by 32% while mean Dice decreased from 0.840 to 0.830; its measured latency and the practical relevance of this trade-off remain to be evaluated.

**B 是目前证据支持的肯定陈述。**数值计算明确，指向也明确；不必为了“学术谨慎”改成 *may reduce*。若评测只含某输入尺寸，最好同时说明这一范围。

**C 错在机制和普适性。**没有病例级路由对照，也没有跨病灶分析，更没有“每个病灶最佳”的数据。可以写：

> These results motivate further study of whether adaptive routing offers a more favorable trade-off than fixed routing for particular patient groups.

## 任务 2：三种有价值但不等价的叙事

**版本 A · 分割问题优先**

> In the hypothetical segmentation evaluation, model B achieved a mean Dice of 0.830, compared with 0.840 for baseline A. This decrease requires closer examination before judging its practical significance, particularly because lesion-level and case-level errors were not reported. At the same time, B reduced estimated computation from 100 to 68 GFLOPs. The resulting trade-off raises a more specific question: which image conditions, if any, permit lower computation without disproportionate errors in clinically relevant regions?

**版本 B · 计算预算优先**

> Under the specified inference-computation protocol, model B used 68 rather than 100 GFLOPs per case, representing a 32% reduction relative to baseline A. Mean Dice was 0.830 rather than 0.840. This result offers a concrete starting point for studying computation–quality trade-offs, but it does not establish faster execution on deployed hardware. Measurements of end-to-end latency, memory use, and the distribution of prediction errors would determine whether the estimated operation reduction translates into a useful system-level advantage.

**版本 C · 科学问题优先**

> Multimodal segmentation systems must decide how computational resources support different sources of evidence. A fixed policy is one possible choice, but whether it remains efficient across cases is an open question rather than an established failure. In the hypothetical comparison, model B reduced estimated operations from 100 to 68 GFLOPs, while mean Dice declined from 0.840 to 0.830. These observations motivate controlled experiments on case-conditioned allocation, not a claim that such allocation has already been shown to be optimal.

A 的中心是**误差在何处发生**；B 的中心是**预算如何转化为部署收益**；C 的中心是**什么新关系值得检验**。同一事实，不同论证路线。若读者会误以为“Dice 一样”“已实时部署”或“已证明动态决策机制”，就超过了可接受的强调。

## 任务 3：教育情境的两种定位

**教师视角**

> The hypothetical course results suggest that AI-assisted feedback can help students produce more fluent final assignments. That is a useful classroom outcome, even though it does not establish greater independent writing ability. Teachers could treat the tool as a source of formative feedback while adding unassisted tasks that reveal what students can plan, argue, and revise on their own. Such assessments would separate the quality of assisted work from retained learning.

**课程设计视角**

> Higher fluency scores on AI-assisted assignments do not automatically indicate stronger independent reasoning. In the hypothetical course, students produced more polished work, but their ability to write without assistance was not assessed. Rather than rejecting AI feedback altogether, curriculum designers could compare assisted practice with delayed, unassisted writing on new prompts. This would preserve the potential value of feedback while collecting evidence about transferable learning.

两者都允许做**行动建议**，并且把建议与已测结论清晰区分。强说服力并不来自夸大结果，而是选择了读者正在努力解决的问题。

## 最终检查

一项叙事选择有效，应满足：**重要事实不丢失、研究问题明确、信息先后服务于读者判断、不把推测写成结果**。不要求每个版本平均陈述所有利弊；要求读者形成的核心结论仍然正确。
