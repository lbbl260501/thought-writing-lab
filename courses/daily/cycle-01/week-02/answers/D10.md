# D10 · Claim–Ground–Warrant 讲解与答案

本课所有研究数字均为虚构教学情境；范例提供论证结构，而不声称结果已复现。不同句序可以成立，只要证据和主张的角色明确。

## 任务 1：三条缺失的桥梁

**A · 参数量 → 术中可靠性**。已知只是一项模型规模描述。缺失推理桥梁包括参数量与硬件时延的关系，以及延迟与定位稳定性、失败恢复、安全性的关系。竞争解释：更小的网络可能使用访存效率低的算子或对分布偏移更敏感。

> The model contains fewer parameters than the comparator. Its end-to-end latency and reliability during surgery require separate evaluation.

**B · 辅助条件下流畅 → 独立推理**。前者是有工具时的成品属性，后者是无工具条件下的能力。竞争解释：工具修正了文句，但学生未掌握论证方法；原本更有能力的人更积极使用工具。

> Students receiving AI feedback produced more fluent drafts under the evaluated conditions. Whether they can construct arguments independently without the tool remains unknown.

**C · 损失下降 → 捕获解剖约束**。训练或验证损失下降是观察，却不足以识别工作机制。竞争解释：正则化提升了平滑性、改善优化或改变有效容量，而非专门学习解剖。可以写：

> Introducing the regularizer lowered the measured loss, but this result alone does not establish which anatomical properties the model represents.

这些示例不仅要削弱强句，更要指向能澄清机制的实验。

## 任务 2：一种四句论证链

> In a hypothetical comparison under a matched nominal FLOPs budget, adaptive routing obtained a mean Dice of 0.842, whereas fixed routing obtained 0.835. If routing decisions use task-relevant case information, such differences could persist against suitably matched nonadaptive policies. These observations therefore motivate, but do not establish, the claim that case-dependent allocation may be beneficial under the evaluated conditions. Random-routing controls, repeated runs, and independent measures of modality contribution remain necessary to test the proposed explanation.

这里第二句是 Warrant 与**可检验的条件前提**，不是已完成结果；第四句承认还未评估的替代。只写“0.842 大于 0.835，therefore 动态路由优越”会让读者无法判断机制与稳健性。

## 任务 3：竞争解释和对照

H1：
> We hypothesize that the routing policy exploits case-dependent image information relevant to segmentation performance.

H2：
> An alternative explanation is that the gain results from greater representational capacity or optimization differences rather than informative case selection.

**一种有意义的实验**：比较自适应路由、随机路由和始终固定路由，在尽可能匹配参数量、训练数据、训练轮次及名义 FLOPs 的条件下改变“决策是否使用病例内容”。考虑多随机种子、同一测试病例和病灶子组，以免一个平均 Dice 混淆解释。

**可能反驳 H1 的结果**：随机路由在匹配容量和计算预算后获得相近表现，或路由选择与病例特征/独立模态干预收益没有稳定关联。这不会自动证明 H2 为真，但削弱“有效病例条件化决策是主要机制”的解释。

## 迁移示例（教育，假设情境）

> In a hypothetical classroom study, students who used an AI writing assistant submitted more fluent essays than those who did not. This difference does not by itself show that the tool improved independent reasoning, because students' prior ability and time spent revising may differ. A stronger claim would require a suitable comparison and an unassisted writing assessment on unfamiliar topics. Delayed performance would help distinguish immediate output improvement from retained ability.

## 评估时不要把三项合成一个笼统分数

语言正确但 Warrant 不成立，意味着**准确性可能高、科学推理分较低**。给自己一项检查：任意删掉段落中的 *therefore*，你能否用具体的实验前提重新接上两句？不能就继续修桥。
