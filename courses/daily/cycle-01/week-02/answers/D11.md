# D11 · 关于证据、语气与边界的解释

本课数据均为假设情境；评分以主张与证据是否匹配为准，而不是统计 *may/might* 的数量。

## 任务 1：哪些词该用，哪些不该用

A 过度模糊已经给定的观测。可改为：  
> Model A achieved a mean Dice of 0.842 on the specified test set.

B 由单中心观察直接外推“所有患者”。应收窄为：  
> Model A obtained a higher mean Dice than B in the evaluated cohort; generalization to other patient populations remains untested.

C 将指标差异升级成机制结论。可写：  
> The observed difference motivates testing whether A uses case-dependent modality information more effectively.

D 是目前较可靠的比较表述：它指定了**评价条件下的数值差异**。但没有方差或区间、检验方案和重复实验，不能声称统计显著，也不等于临床意义。

## 任务 2：可行示范（约 100 词）

> In a hypothetical retrospective single-center evaluation, the model achieved a mean Dice of 0.86, whereas removing the module reduced the score to 0.84 under the same data split. This observation is consistent with the module contributing useful information, but it does not establish the mechanism responsible for the difference. Because the evaluation used only one split, the stability of the result remains uncertain. Performance on external data and small lesions was also not assessed. Repeated controlled ablations across independent datasets, accompanied by lesion-size analyses, would help determine whether the gain is reproducible and under which conditions it occurs.

**为何不是废话**：第一句把观测说清楚；第二句揭示还未证实的机制；第三、四句限定具体未知项；最后一句提出可反驳“普适有效”的验证。

## 任务 3：两种体裁的允许差别

**一般论说型（原创教学表达）**

> AI writing tools may help students produce clearer essays by providing immediate feedback on wording and organization. Yet a more polished assignment is not necessarily evidence that students have developed the ability to plan and revise arguments independently. Schools can value these tools for the support they provide while still assessing learning without assistance. A delayed writing task on a new topic would offer better evidence of retained ability than a comparison of assisted essays alone.

**研究型句群**

> In the hypothetical semester-long observation, average scores for AI-assisted essays increased. This result describes the quality of submissions produced with access to feedback, not necessarily retained independent writing ability. Other explanations include additional revision opportunities or prior differences among students. An unassisted delayed assessment with suitable controls would be needed to evaluate the learning claim.

前者可以在价值判断中提出“学校可以”，因为它是可争论的规范性建议；后者必须区分已记录的输出与尚未检验的心理能力。**规范性主张与实证性主张并非同一种证据负担**。

## 今天该形成的习惯

- 确切观测不需假装模糊：*was measured*, *achieved*, *was observed*。
- 机制未检验时不可提前确证：*may reflect*, *one possible explanation*。
- 外部推广需明确范围：*in the evaluated cohort*, *under the specified conditions*。
- 决策价值另有证据门槛：现实部署还需相应环境与风险评估。

自评时检查：你究竟缩小了**对象、证据范围、机制解释还是行动建议**？若说不清楚，谨慎词可能只是装饰。
