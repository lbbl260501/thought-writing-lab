# D09 · 参考答案与比较框架核查

本页所用数值和模型均为虚构教学情境，不能作为真实论文结果引用。**正确答案不止一种**，关键是比较坐标明确、信息不丢失。

## 任务 1：拆解不完整比较

A. *outperforms* 未说明对照模型、指标、测试集和差异；*significantly* 在论文中容易暗示统计检验。可写：  
> On the specified test set, the model achieved a higher mean Dice than baseline A.

前提是确实提供了测试集与数值；若没有，应改写成**计划比较的研究问题**而非结果句。

B. *preserves accuracy* 至少需要定义“preserve”的判据及不确定性，不能凭 0.830 对 0.840 宣称等价；*resources* 是 FLOPs、显存、延迟还是能耗？  
> Method B reduced estimated FLOPs from 100 to 68 GFLOPs, while mean Dice decreased from 0.840 to 0.830.

C. *practical* 可能涉及导航误差、系统延迟、流程中断、临床安全性、操作者负担和故障恢复。仅有 FLOPs 无法判断：  
> The measured reduction in computational operations motivates further evaluation of end-to-end latency and navigation accuracy under operating-room conditions.

## 任务 2：三种可成立的比较

**质量视角**

> Baseline A reached a mean Dice of 0.840, whereas method B reached 0.830 on the same test set. The latter required 68 rather than 100 GFLOPs per case, but the Dice difference should be examined at the patient and lesion levels before claiming negligible performance loss.

**预算视角**

> Under the specified computation protocol, method B used 68 GFLOPs per case, compared with 100 GFLOPs for baseline A. This 32% reduction was accompanied by a decrease in mean Dice from 0.840 to 0.830, leaving a computation–quality trade-off for further evaluation.

**部署视角**

> Method B reduced the estimated operation count from 100 to 68 GFLOPs but also reduced mean Dice from 0.840 to 0.830. Because inference latency and missing-modality robustness were not measured, the present comparison does not establish that B is preferable for clinical deployment.

三段的信息排序不同，但都保留好坏两个方向。不要把它们中的任何一段简化成“B without compromising accuracy”。

## 任务 3：一种英文论证

> The reported F1 scores do not establish which model handles seasonal pseudo-changes better, because P and Q were evaluated on different datasets with different acquisition conditions and labeling protocols. A fair comparison would use a common definition of real and pseudo-change, matched evaluation splits, and the same metric computation. It should report performance across seasonal conditions and separate false positives due to appearance changes from missed genuine changes. Only after this comparison could we assess whether either model shows stronger robustness to seasonal variation.

平均 F1 相同依然可能掩盖**类别差异、错误类型与环境子组差异**。如果“鲁棒”指季节变化下的退化幅度，应按 D08 先定义干预及测量方式，再按 D09 设置比较坐标。

## 自我评分

- 准确性：指标与单位是否吻合？32% 是根据 (100−68)/100，而不是 Dice 的相对变化。
- 清晰性：读者能否找出四个比较元素？
- 科学推理：是否把未测延迟、未建置信区间或不同数据集之间的差别错误地变成结论？
- 迁移：是否在遥感题里重新设计公平比较，而不是机械替换 PET/CT 术语？
