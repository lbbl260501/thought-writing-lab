# Learner-facing courses

Courses are composed from curated knowledge cards, language material and exercises, but should read like rich teaching material rather than internal technical reports.

A lesson typically includes:
1. A motivating problem and why it matters.
2. Carefully developed concepts and worked examples.
3. Annotated source-based examples and contrasting choices.
4. Structured micro exercises.
5. Transfer into a research-paper or theoretical-construction task.
6. Worked solutions, feedback rubrics, and follow-up reading.

Keep audits, data counts and project management outside student-facing PDFs.
