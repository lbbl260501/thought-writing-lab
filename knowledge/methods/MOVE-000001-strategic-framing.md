# MOVE-000001 — Strategic framing / 策略性研究框架

## Problem
One study may yield several defensible claims, each requiring different evidence and serving different readers. A sequential report of all experimental steps does not automatically communicate research value.

## Actionable operation
1. Inventory supported findings, uncertainty and trade-offs.
2. Generate multiple possible contributions: empirical performance, new problem definition, methodological principle, or conceptual explanation.
3. For each contribution, define the relevant evaluation standard and expected reader questions.
4. Choose a central claim matched to available evidence.
5. Reorder motivation, comparisons, results and caveats around that claim.
6. Identify which additional experiments are needed to strengthen the selected framing.

## English applications
Practice topic sentences, controlled concession, emphasis, contrast, limitation placement and section-level information order.

## Scientific applications
Compare research questions under different resource budgets and clinical contexts; identify whether the new evaluation standard is substantively relevant.

## Boundary
Strategic emphasis is legitimate; results cannot be invented, and core conclusions should not imply more evidence than has been established.
