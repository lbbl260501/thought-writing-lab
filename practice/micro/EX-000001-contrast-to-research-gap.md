# EX-000001 — 从对比到可研究的问题

- Move: [MOVE-000001](../../knowledge/methods/MOVE-000001-strategic-framing.md)
- Difficulty: intermediate / advanced
- Scales: English sentence and paragraph

## A. Short English transformation
Input:
> Online education offers flexibility. It may not provide equal access to feedback.

Rewrite as a single sentence using `although`. Preserve the uncertainty signaled by `may`.

## B. Scientific continuation
Input:
> Multimodal fusion can integrate complementary information.

Write two follow-up sentences. First identify a specific conditional challenge; then formulate a researchable question. Avoid generic phrases such as "many limitations" without defining them.

## C. Paragraph transfer
Write a 100–150-word paragraph using four moves:
research value → existing capabilities → a context-specific tension → a testable question.

## Evaluation
- Does the logical relationship remain accurate?
- Is the main clause carrying the intended scientific emphasis?
- Are key concepts defined rather than treated as vague labels?
- Is the question testable with a plausible experiment?
- Are claims calibrated to the evidence supplied?

## Model answer (A)
Although online education offers flexibility, it may not provide equal access to feedback.

## Extended reflection
Produce a different version for an exam essay and for a research introduction, then explain why the rhetorical emphasis changes.
